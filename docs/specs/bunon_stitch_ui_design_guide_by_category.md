**Core Design & Button Navigation Rules to Paste into Stitch**

* **Colour ratio:** 99% Neutral, 1% Marigold: Screens are almost entirely white, black, and grey. Marigold (\#FFB020 light / \#FFC24D dark) is reserved strictly for small completion marks (done task node, reached milestone knot, certificate seal). Never use Marigold for buttons, banners, backgrounds, text, or 'in-progress' dots. Done is Marigold, never green.  
* **One solid button rule:** Strictly ONE solid black button (\#1A1A1B fill, white text) per screen. Every other button on the screen must be a 1px hairline outline or soft neutral fill (\#EDEEF0) or plain text link.  
* **Softness & geometry:** Cards use 20px rounded corners (1px border \#E3E5E8, 24px internal padding, 16px side margins, 16px gap between cards, zero card shadows). Bottom sheets use 28px top rounded corners with one soft shadow. Buttons and chips are full-round pills (9999px). Knots are circular.  
* **Typography:** Use one clean sans-serif font family that supports both Bangla and English (Noto Sans Bengali or Hind Siliguri, matching the clean style of the bKash and My Robi apps; line height 1.6 for Bangla). Use sentence case everywhere. Do NOT use JetBrains Mono or uppercase monospace labels.  
* **The Thread (visual motif): One continuous vertical or horizontal line connects the logo, progress bar, streak knot, timeline spine, and Journey Card border. Keep 'The Thread' purely visual.**  
* **Banned words (never generate): NEVER use the words 'verified', 'signed hash', 'cryptographically anchored', 'artisan credential', 'permanent record', 'certificate', 'completion certificate', 'loom', 'warp', 'weft', 'spine', 'sprint', 'woven', 'Blocked by dependencies', or 'New instant'. Use plain words: Milestone, Your path, Done, Proof, Saved, Journey Card.**  
* **Banned words (never generate):** NEVER use the words 'verified', 'signed hash', 'cryptographically anchored', 'artisan credential', 'permanent record', 'loom', 'warp', 'weft', 'spine', 'sprint', 'woven', 'Blocked by dependencies', or 'New instant'. Use plain words: Milestone, Your path, Done, Proof, Saved, Completion Certificate.
