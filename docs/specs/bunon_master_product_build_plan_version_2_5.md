**1\. Product Identity, Positioning & Guiding Principles**

* **App Name:** Bunon (বুনন — from the Bangla word for weaving). (Decided)  
* **Tagline:** Make Every Day Count (placed under the Thread 'B' logo). (Decided)  
* **The Idea in One Sentence: An adaptive goal-completion and comeback companion that knows your long-term goal (4 weeks to 12 months), learns how you actually live and work, and guides what to do next without guilt or rigid schedules. You prove each finished outcome with evidence, and your journey becomes a permanent timeline and a free, shareable Journey Card. (Decided)**  
* **Core Differentiator — Person-First Adaptive Life Planning (Built for People Who Start Strong, Then Fade):** Most productivity apps demand that users reorganize their lives around a rigid calendar and only learn from users who type long notes. Bunon is engineered specifically for the critical first 4 weeks: it learns the person (not just the goal) across 3 memory scopes ('PERSON', 'DOMAIN', 'GOAL'), computes zero-token behavior signals and a 5-state user state ('STEADY', 'SLIPPING', 'STRUGGLING', 'AHEAD', 'OVERLOADED') in code, separates life interruptions from system/task-trust failures, and proves that today's work is getting the user to their outcome. (Decided)  
* **The 5-Second Test on Today (Task-First 2-Beat Thread):** A stranger shown Today (Screen 22\) for 5 seconds must immediately lock onto ONE clear Task Title (with a compact `'[N] steps ⌄'` step-by-step dropdown right beside it) and the connected `'Snap proof to finish'` camera-first row (no giant black button bar stealing visual attention). To keep Today completely calm and clutter-free, `today_context_line` (e.g., `'Light one today — Mondays are tuition days'`), honest pace/streak, and the 3-knot `'Why this task'` chain live cleanly inside the Hero Card's subtle **`ⓘ`** icon (Sheet 22b), while `'10-minute version'`, `'Skip — too basic'`, and AI rescue options live inside **`'Stuck or short on time?'`** (Sheet 37b). Today uses a **3-Slot Fluid Morph Deck** showing strictly at most 3 task slots at once (1 compressed completed task peeking above, 1 broad active Hero Card in the center, and 1 compressed upcoming task peeking below). Completing a task runs a **2-stage Marigold choreography** (pops Marigold `#FFB020` Done in place for ~600ms so the user clearly sees their progress register, then smoothly compresses upward as the next task rises from below). Pulling down (Mobile elastic drag) or scrolling/pressing `↑` (Desktop) smoothly broadens earlier completed tasks into the full Hero Card layout (with saved proof and `'Mark as not done'` Sheet 22c, which warns that proof will be permanently deleted and syncs `/today`, `/roadmap`, and `/timeline`), accompanied by a floating `'Back to today ↓/↑'` pill. (Decided)  
* **Brand Look & Honesty Rules: 99% neutral (white, black, grey) and 1% Marigold (\#FFB020) strictly for finished marks (done node, reached milestone knot, Journey Card seal). One solid black button per screen. Plain everyday words only—never say evidence is 'verified', 'signed', 'audited', or 'cryptographically anchored', never call the finish card a 'certificate' or 'completion certificate', and never use weaving jargon ('loom', 'warp', 'weft', 'spine', 'sprint'). Mascot is postponed to a later version. (Decided)**
